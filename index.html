03 · The Cultural Experience

ALDEA · The Art of Belonging

An intimate retreat on the Argentine coast. A place where you can be exactly who you are.

7 days · 6 nights6–12 womenChapadmalal, ArgentinaUSD 1,800

Rest, share, and connect, with nothing to prove.

At Tu Portal · Aldea de los Abrazos, a home on the Atlantic coast opens its doors to a small group of women who want to slow down and build honest, conscious connections. This edition is designed especially for lesbian women from the U.S.

“A place where you don't need to be different to belong.”

Circles of sharing and deep listening. Silence, reading and time in nature. Fresh plant-based meals cooked and shared in the village. Writing, art and creativity. Walks along the cliffs, the Energy Forest of Miramar, bonfires, music and laughter.

Created & guided by Mariela Tiboni
Nutritionist · Biology & Spirituality · Founder of Tu Portal. Your bridge in English: Gigi.

About Gigi

A nomad who came home.

I'm from Appalachia. For years I was a professional nomad, working in education and travel across cultures and continents. Now I'm back on my home ground: I work as an interpreter and live in a farmhouse in Floyd, Virginia.

Everything I offer comes from the same place: helping people feel at home, in a language, in a house, or in a community. Whether you need a hand with Spanish or English, a peaceful room in the mountains, or a journey to the Argentine coast, I'd love to walk alongside you.
